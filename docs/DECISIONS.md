- 2026-10-22: Device hash is SHA-256 of `deviceId + ":" + secret`. Reason: the spec says id plus secret; the separator keeps the two parts distinct.
- 2026-10-22: Rule T: a `confirm_topup` counts only if it is newer than the newest patient top-up report. A `correct_topup` is overridden when 2 or more newer reports differ from it after rounding to GH¢ 5; the amount then shown is the median of those newer reports. Reason: the spec says "newer" and "disagree" without defining them.
