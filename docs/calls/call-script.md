# Veriq verification call: script

One call, about 3 minutes. Be polite and brief. Never ask for a patient's name, and never give one.

## Before you dial

- Open **/admin/calls/new** and find the facility, or have the paper sheet (`calls-template.csv`) ready.
- Note the time you dial.

## 1. Greeting

> Good morning. My name is ___. I'm calling from Veriq Health. Is this [facility name]?

If it's the wrong number, say sorry and end the call. Record nothing.

## 2. Who we are

> Veriq is a free service that helps patients in Accra find out which hospitals accept their insurance card, before they travel. We're checking our list with each facility. May I ask you four quick questions? It takes about three minutes.

If they say no or ask you to call back, thank them and ask when is a good time. Record nothing yet.

> Thank you. Who am I speaking with? Just your role, for example front desk or records.

Write the role only (for example "Front desk"), never the person's name.

## 3. The questions, one card at a time

Ask about **NHIS first**, then each private card in the table: Adom Care, Cosmopolitan Health, Prudential and Sika Health.

> Do you accept [card] for outpatient visits today?

Mark one answer:

| They say | Mark |
| --- | --- |
| "Yes" | **Accepts** |
| "No" | **Does not accept** |
| "Yes, but patients pay for some things" (for example scans or drugs) | **Partial**, with a short note such as "top-up for scans" |
| They don't know, or you didn't ask | **Not asked** |

If they say "partial", ask:

> What do patients usually pay extra for?

Write a few words. No amounts are needed.

## 4. If there's time (optional)

> Which of these do you offer: antenatal care, delivery, emergency, scans, lab tests, a doctor's consultation, dental?

> What are your usual opening hours?

> What number should patients call to reach you?

Note the number you reached. It's saved on the facility, so patients can call it.

## 5. Polite close

> Thank you very much. That's all. Patients will see your answer on Veriq, marked "Veriq call" with today's date. If anything changes, your staff can call us back. Have a good day.

## After the call

Record it straight away at **/admin/calls/new**, or on the paper sheet with the exact time. For each card, "Not asked" is the default: change it only for the cards they answered.
