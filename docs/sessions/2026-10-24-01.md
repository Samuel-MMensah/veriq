## How to check it yourself
1. Run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:3000/h/check, then tap "Demo login". To avoid changing the live demo data, run with `VERIQ_DATA=local`.
2. Tap Antenatal clinic → Closed, then "Confirm and publish". You see "Published at … Patients see it now."
3. Open http://localhost:3000/results?service=antenatal&card=nhis&area=dansoman. Akoma shows "Antenatal clinic: Closed · updated by hospital just now".
4. Open http://localhost:3000/i/network and tap "Demo login": 23 members refused at 6 hospitals, with Pinecrest first.
