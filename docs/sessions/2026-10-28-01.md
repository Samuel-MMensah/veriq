# Session 2026-10-28-01: Prompt 10c, Look and copy polish

## Summary
All four sides (patients, hospital staff, insurers, admin) now share one calm design system:
- four type sizes and three weights;
- colour for meaning only;
- one card, badge and button style;
- one staff shell;
- a centred sign-in card.

About 70 pieces of on-screen text were cut or shortened, and the explanations moved to /how-we-check. No rule, data, privacy behaviour or feature changed, and every rule text from SPEC section 4 keeps its exact words. All 132 e2e and 507 unit tests pass.
