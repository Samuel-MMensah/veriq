# Session 2026-10-22-11: Prompt 6, Cards, emergency, help

## Summary
People can now save their cards on the phone (last 4 digits only), and the app starts every search with them and reminds them a month before their NHIS card expires. Emergency mode lists the nearest emergency units by status and distance only, with 112 and 193 one tap away, and shows the person's cards as notes that never change the list. Phones without data can use a tested USSD menu that answers by SMS (printed to the console until an aggregator is connected).

## How to check it yourself
1. Run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:3000/cards. Add an NHIS card: last 4 digits, a name, an expiry month and "Kofi (son)". Then add a Cosmopolitan card.
2. Open Find: your NHIS card is first ("Saved card · …") and already chosen.
3. Open http://localhost:3000/emergency: Akoma, Westhills, then Pinecrest (full). Each shows what your cards mean there; the order doesn't change if you remove a card.
4. Try USSD from PowerShell: `curl.exe -X POST http://localhost:3000/api/ussd -d "phoneNumber=+233200000000&text=1*1*1*1"`, then look for "[SMS stub]" in the `npm run dev` window.

## Problems and risks
- `/api/ussd` accepts requests from anyone; Phase 2 must check that calls come from the aggregator (a shared secret or IP allowlist). Rule A's burst holding limits fake refusals until then: medium
- The USSD report path uses the phone number's hash as the device id, so a person who reports by USSD and in the app counts as two devices: low
- The cards screen asks "Remove?" with the browser's confirm box, which looks plain on some phones: low
